ingredients:
  - 250 g plain flour, plus extra for dusting
  - 1 tsp baking powder
  - ½ tsp fine salt
  - 200 g natural yoghurt
  - 1 tbsp olive oil

1. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl. Add the yoghurt and the oil and bring together
   into a soft dough. Knead for a minute, cover, and rest for 15 minutes.
2. Divide into six. On a floured surface roll each piece to the thickness of a coin.
3. Heat a dry, heavy pan until hot. Cook each flatbread for about 2 minutes a side, until puffed
   and marked.
