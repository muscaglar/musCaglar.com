Fırın makarnası is oven pasta, also called pastitsio. Mine has one thick layer of pasta in two halves: one half is pure pasta, the other is mixed in with mince. The mince layer is distinct from that of a lasagne, with mint and parsley forward. The béchamel should not be too heavy with cheese: its notes should be cream and milk, with nutmeg.
