1. Mix everything well. No machine is needed.
2. Divide into five small balls.
3. Rest in the freezer for 10 to 15 min.
4. Bake for 14 min at 150 °C fan.
5. Once they have cooled, dust the tops with icing sugar.

## Notes

The quantities are for five biscuits.
