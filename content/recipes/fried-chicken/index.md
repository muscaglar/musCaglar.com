My notes for this give the order of work and no quantities.

1. Mix equal parts of cornstarch and flour with some milk to get a thick batter. Lumps are fine.
2. Add a bit of baking powder to the batter.
3. Coat the chicken in the spice rub, with a bit of cornstarch.
4. Prepare another pan of salt, cornstarch, flour and a bit of baking powder. This is the dry mix.
5. Dredge the spiced chicken into the batter, all of it at once.
6. Take the pieces out of the batter and pat them into the dry mix.
7. Fry.
8. Drain on kitchen paper and salt.

## Notes

The spice rub is of your choice. The one here is the one I chose.
