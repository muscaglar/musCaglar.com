1. Dissolve the stock cube fully in the hot water.
2. Wash the rice thoroughly in a strainer, then drain it.
3. Put the oil in a saucepan with the vermicelli and heat it at 8 to 9 on my hob until the vermicelli is brown.
4. Add the drained rice, then pour in the stock.
5. Once it boils, turn the heat down to 3, put the lid on and set a timer for 10 min.
6. When the timer goes off, lift the lid, quickly lay a clean tea towel over the pan and put the lid back on top of it.
7. Wait at least 3 to 4 min. Give the rice a mix before serving.

## Notes

The water and the rice are measured with the same cup: two of water to one of rice.
