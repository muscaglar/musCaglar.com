1. Thaw the frozen bananas in the microwave for 6 to 8 min. Keep the liquid: it goes in.
2. Soak the raisins in hot water with the 3 teabags. Keep the water: it goes in too.
3. Melt the butter.

5. Split across two tins.
6. Bake for 60 min at 180 °C.
