## Cake

1. Heat the oven to 150 °C fan.
2. Prepare the tin: coat it in butter.
3. Sift the flour and the baking powder into a bowl.
4. Separate the egg whites from the yolks.
5. In a bowl, beat the egg whites to soft peaks.
6. In another bowl, cream the sugar and the egg yolks for 2 to 3 min on high speed.
7. On low speed, add the milk and the flour and baking powder mix. Mix only until just incorporated.
8. Fold in the egg whites. Keep the air in, but make sure no white streaks remain.
9. Bake for 45 min.
10. Cool for 2 h.

## Soak

1. Poke loads of holes in the cake.
2. Combine the milks for the soak.
3. Ladle the soak over the cake. Wait patiently for it to be fully absorbed before adding more.
4. Cover with cling film and leave in the fridge overnight to soak.

## Topping: Italian meringue

Optional, and not the one I prefer.

1. Put the egg whites in the clean bowl of a stand mixer. If you like, first add 1 tsp of lemon juice to the bowl and wipe it away, or add ½ tsp of cream of tartar to the egg whites once they are frothy. Or do neither.
2. Whip on medium until frothy.
3. Meanwhile, heat the sugar and the water in a pan over a high heat, with a sugar thermometer. Boil until it reaches 121 °C.
4. With the mixer still on medium, slowly stream the hot sugar syrup into the egg whites. Avoid hitting the beaters.
5. Turn the mixer to high and whip until the bowl is at room temperature (10 min).
6. Pipe or spoon the meringue on to the sponge and sprinkle it with cinnamon. Torch parts of the meringue if you like.
