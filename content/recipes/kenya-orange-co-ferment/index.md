This is the design as it stands, not yet the profile I will keep. It has not been roasted as written, and every number is provisional until first crack has been heard at this batch size. The coffee is meant to come out light to medium.

All temperatures are those of the exhaust air at the outlet of the drum. They are not the temperature of the beans.

1. Start cold: the display reads below 40 °C.
2. Set 244 °C, fan 2, drum 2 and the timer to 11:30. Start, and charge the coffee at once.
3. At 3:20, when the roaster beeps at 200 °C, set fan 3.
4. Hold 244 °C until first crack. Do not cut the heat before it.
5. Listen from 8:00. First crack is expected between 8:30 and 9:45.
6. At first crack, set 235 °C.
7. Cool 1 min 20 s after first crack.

The timer is the backstop. If first crack is missed, the roaster cools by itself at 11:30.

## What to expect

| Time | Exhaust air |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | 45 °C |
| 1:00 | 127 °C |
| 2:00 | 168 °C |
| 3:20 | 200 °C |
| 5:00 | 230 °C |
| From about 6:50 to first crack, at about 9:15 | 244 °C |
| To the cool, at about 10:35 | 236 to 235 °C |

## The coffee

Fermented for 96 hours without air, with koji and orange, and grown at about 1,800 m. The importer gives its moisture as 11.1 % and its water activity as 0.61.

## Tasting

As filter: 60 g to 900 ml at 93 °C, first on day 5 after roasting and again between days 7 and 10. As espresso: 20 g in and 40 g out in 25 to 28 s, between days 10 and 14.

## Where it stands

Four roasts so far, and none of them is the one to share. At 250 g, one came out dark, with a fifth of its weight lost. The design above was made after that one. The next came out light and uneven, with 12 % lost, and did not follow the design.

The roaster is driven by [Kahve](../../projects/coffee-roaster/), software of my own.
